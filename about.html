Our Story

Bapir Productions didn't start in a boardroom; it started in a high school TV Production class back in 2011. However, the spark was ignited much earlier—I began producing videos as an amateur at the age of 13.

What started as a childhood passion has evolved into a full-scale production house specializing in independent films, music videos, and documentaries. We believe every frame should tell a story.
